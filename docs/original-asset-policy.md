# Original asset policy

Chardin accepts only assets that are either created for the project or accompanied by documented rights permitting repository distribution and product use.

For every future image, model, texture, font, shader, animation, or audio file, record its creator, source, license, modifications, and required attribution beside the asset. Do not import scraped site assets, deployed bundles, clone-repository material, or work with unclear licensing. Do not imitate the distinctive geometry, palette, sequence, writing, or trade dress of a named artwork.

WIL-117 contains no external art assets. Its Planet, grass, Traveler, and palette are authored in code from stock Three.js geometry primitives.
